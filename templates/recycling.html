Recycling Tips

Simple steps to help protect our local community and environment.

  • Separate waste properly: Keep organic waste, plastics, and papers in distinct bins.
  • Recycle plastics and paper: Clean them out before throwing them into the recycling bin.
  • Reduce plastic use: Switch to reusable bags, bottles, and containers.
  • Dispose of electronics properly: Take e-waste to designated collection points instead of regular trash.
